Fueled by coffee?

Who we are

We are a community of coffee lovers who gather to explore, taste, and celebrate everything coffee. From cupping sessions and brewing workshops to café crawls and latte art competitions, we bring together enthusiasts of all levels — whether you're a casual drinker or a full-on coffee geek. If coffee is your passion, you've found your people.

"I never thought I'd find my people, but this club proved me wrong. We don't just drink coffee — we obsess over it together. Best decision I ever made."

— Marco, member since 2022

What we do

From hands-on brewing workshops to cupping sessions and café crawls, we keep things interesting all year round. Whether you're learning latte art for the first time or deep-diving into single-origin roasts, there's always something brewing for every level of enthusiasm.
